Dance nights for the spaces we share

Common Steps is a nonprofit that produces Argentine tango socials in Cambridge and Somerville. We bring the DJ, the beginner lesson, and the dancers. A lobby, a rooftop, or a commons brings the floor.

What a night involves

We bring

  • Event production and artistic direction
  • A DJ and a beginner-friendly lesson
  • Ticketing, registration, check-in, and an attendance report afterward
  • Promotion through our email list, local calendars, social media, and tango networks
  • Hosts and volunteers on the floor
  • A code of conduct and consent-based dance guidelines
  • Speakers, when the house system isn't a fit

The venue provides

  • A clear floor, with time to set up and reset
  • Help moving furniture before and after
  • A place on your calendar and in your channels
  • Two tables: one for the DJ, one for check-in
  • House sound, if you have it

A sample Friday evening

  1. 5:30 pmDoors open and the DJ starts
  2. 6:00 pmBeginner lesson, 30 minutes. No partner or experience needed.
  3. 6:30 pmSocial dancing
  4. 8:30 pmLast song, then we reset the room

Team

Samuel Galler

Sam is a Greater Boston tango organizer who produces recurring community events and ticketed performances. He previously served as an officer of the MIT Tango Club and is the Arts & Entertainment Chair for the MIT Club of Boston.

Paloma Rodríguez

Paloma has danced Argentine tango for more than 20 years, starting in Chile in 2005. She moved to Buenos Aires in 2021 and trained with internationally known tango artists. In 2023 she joined Sensaciones de Tango at Café Tortoni, working in show operations, guest reception, and ticket sales while helping organize milongas. In 2025 she co-organized the Miami Sunshine Tango Festival.

On the floor

  • No partner or dance experience needed. Our socials include a beginner lesson.
  • Invitations are respectful and consent-based. Saying no is always fine.
  • A code of conduct covers respect, anti-harassment, and non-discrimination.
  • Accessible seating and clear paths, for dancers and for people watching.
